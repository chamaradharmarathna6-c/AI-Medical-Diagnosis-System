Your Previous Assessments

This page allows you to review your previous AI preliminary assessment results and their current review status.

Understanding Your History

  • Date & Time: Shows when the assessment was created.
  • Condition: Shows the health condition assessed.
  • Prediction: Shows the AI model's preliminary result.
  • Review Status: Shows the current status of the assessment.

Important Notice

Previous AI predictions are for decision-support purposes only. They should not be considered a final medical diagnosis. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice and confirmation.
